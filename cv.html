Biography

Jia Cai, Ph.D., is a professor and doctoral supervisor. He received his Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematics at City University of Hong Kong in October 2009. His doctoral supervisor was Professor Ding-Xuan Zhou of the University of Sydney, Australia, a recipient of an Alexander von Humboldt Foundation award and China’s National Science Fund for Distinguished Young Scholars, and Editor-in-Chief of Analysis and Applications.

He made several research visits to City University of Hong Kong between 2009 and 2015, and visited the University at Albany, State University of New York, from February 2017 to February 2018. From February 2013 to December 2016, he held a President’s Specially Appointed Professorship under Guangdong University of Finance and Economics’ Outstanding Young Faculty program. He was promoted to professor through an exceptional promotion procedure in June 2019 and selected for a Guangdong provincial talent program in December of the same year.

He currently serves as a reviewer for the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Guangdong Natural Science Foundation; is registered in the Ministry of Education’s science and technology management expert database; and reviews projects for the science and technology departments of Hubei, Jiangsu and Heilongjiang provinces. He is a member of the Big Data and Artificial Intelligence Committee and a standing committee member of the Financial Technology and Algorithms Committee of the China Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, an executive council member of the Guangdong Society for Computational Mathematics, and a member of the Big Data Committee of the Guangdong Computer Federation.

He has led or participated in more than 20 projects, including projects under the Young Scientists Fund and General Program of the National Natural Science Foundation of China, a key project of the National Social Science Fund of China, a key project of the National Bureau of Statistics of China, Guangdong Natural Science Foundation projects, Ministry of Education humanities and social sciences projects, and Guangzhou Science and Technology Program projects. He has served as principal investigator on three National Natural Science Foundation of China grants, two of which have been completed.

He has published more than 40 SCI- or EI-indexed papers, including accepted papers, in journals such as Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis, IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems, Neural Networks, Neural Computation, Journal of Multivariate Analysis, Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence, Cognitive Computation, Neurocomputing, Complex & Intelligent Systems, Expert Systems With Applications, and Science China (Chinese and English editions), as well as at computer science conferences including IJCNN, ICANN, ICONIP, ICDM, ECML PKDD and PRICAI.
