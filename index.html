About Me

I am a professor and doctoral supervisor at Guangdong University of Finance and Economics. I received my Ph.D. from the Department of Mathematics at City University of Hong Kong in October 2009, under the supervision of Professor Ding-Xuan Zhou.

I made several research visits to City University of Hong Kong between 2009 and 2015, and visited the University at Albany, State University of New York, from February 2017 to February 2018. I was appointed a President’s Specially Appointed Professor under the University’s Outstanding Young Faculty program from February 2013 to December 2016, and promoted to full professor through an exceptional promotion procedure in June 2019. I was selected for a Guangdong provincial talent program in December 2019.

I have led or participated in more than 20 research projects, including three grants from the National Natural Science Foundation of China as principal investigator. My work has resulted in more than 40 SCI- or EI-indexed papers, including accepted papers.

Research

  • Statistical learning and correlation analysis. Kernel methods, sparse learning, canonical correlation analysis and multi-view learning.
  • Graph representation learning. Graph neural networks, heterogeneous graphs, graph contrastive learning and spectral filtering.
  • Multimodal retrieval and generation. Cross-modal and sketch-based image retrieval, and retrieval-augmented generation with knowledge and hypergraphs.
  • Data analysis and optimization. Financial data analysis, stock movement prediction, image segmentation and nature-inspired optimization.
